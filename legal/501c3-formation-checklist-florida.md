## Phase 1 — Form the Florida nonprofit corporation

**Time:** 1–2 weeks · **Cost:** $70 (FL filing fee) + optional registered agent service ~$125/yr

- [ ] **Choose and verify the corporate name**
      "Soul & Sentence" — search availability at [sunbiz.org](https://sunbiz.org). Avoid names that collide with existing Florida corporations. Reserve for 120 days if not filing immediately ($35).
- [ ] **Draft Articles of Incorporation for a nonprofit corporation**
      Required language for 501(c)(3) qualification:
  - Statement of exempt purpose (educational, literary, charitable) citing IRC §501(c)(3)
  - Dissolution clause assigning remaining assets to another 501(c)(3) or government entity
  - No part of the net earnings benefits private shareholders or individuals
  - No substantial political or lobbying activity
- [ ] **File Articles of Incorporation with the Florida Department of State**
      Online filing at [dos.fl.gov/sunbiz](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/). Fee: $70. Approval usually within 5 business days.

## Phase 2 — Federal tax-exempt recognition

**Time:** 3–12 months (IRS backlog) · **Cost:** $275 (Form 1023-EZ) or $600 (Form 1023)

- [ ] **Determine which form to file:**
      - **Form 1023-EZ** — if projected annual gross receipts ≤ $50,000 in each of next 3 years AND assets ≤ $250,000. Fee $275. Most nonprofits this size qualify.
      - **Form 1023 (long form)** — if exceeding the EZ thresholds. Fee $600. 28 pages + schedules.
- [ ] **Pre-check eligibility for 1023-EZ** at [IRS Form 1023-EZ Eligibility Worksheet](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1023-ez)
      Soul & Sentence likely qualifies for EZ in Year 1.
- [ ] **Gather documentation:**
  - EIN confirmation letter (CP 575 or 147C)
  - Articles of Incorporation (filed copy)
  - Bylaws
  - Conflict of Interest Policy (IRS expects one — template in Appendix A of Form 1023 instructions)
  - Three-year budget projection
  - List of officers and directors with compensation (none, if all volunteers)
- [ ] **File Form 1023-EZ online** at [pay.gov](https://www.pay.gov)
      Typical approval: 2–4 weeks for EZ, 3–12 months for long form.
- [ ] **Receive IRS Determination Letter** (Form 5602)
      This is your 501(c)(3) recognition. Scan and save multiple copies. Every grant application, every donor, every bank will ask for it.

**Critical:** Donations made *before* the determination letter are retroactively deductible back to the date of incorporation **if** the 1023 is filed within 27 months of incorporation. After 27 months, deductibility starts on the date of filing.

## Phase 3 — Florida state tax exemption and registration

**Time:** 1–3 weeks · **Cost:** $10 charitable registration + $0 tax exemption

- [ ] **Apply for Florida sales tax exemption** (Consumer's Certificate of Exemption, Form DR-5)
      Free. Required if S&S will purchase supplies exempt from Florida sales tax.
- [ ] **Register as a charitable organization with the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS)**
      Required if soliciting contributions in Florida. Form [FDACS-10100](https://www.fdacs.gov/Divisions-Offices/Consumer-Services/Business-Services/Solicitation-of-Contributions).
      Annual renewal required. $10 for organizations receiving < $5,000; scales up.
- [ ] **Determine if employer tax registration is needed**
      Not required until S&S has paid employees. Volunteers don't count.

## Phase 4 — Operations and compliance

**Ongoing**

- [ ] **Open a business bank account** in the corporation's name (NOT Alex's personal account)
      Required: EIN letter, Articles, Bylaws. Some banks offer free nonprofit accounts (look for local credit unions first).
- [ ] **Set up accounting**
      QuickBooks Nonprofit (~$25/mo) or simpler: Wave Accounting (free). Track every donation, every expense, every grant. Be ready for an audit from day one.
- [ ] **Establish a donation acceptance process**
      - Donor receipt template (for tax-deduction purposes, must include organization name, EIN, donation amount, date, statement "no goods or services were provided in exchange" if true)
      - Payment processor — Stripe (nonprofit discount available), PayPal Giving Fund, or Givebutter (nonprofit-native)
- [ ] **File IRS Form 990-N (e-Postcard)** annually
      Required for small orgs (< $50,000 gross receipts). Simple online form, free. Due 4.5 months after fiscal year end. Miss three years in a row and IRS auto-revokes 501(c)(3) status.
- [ ] **File Florida annual report** at sunbiz.org every year between Jan 1 and May 1.
      Fee: $61.25. Miss it and the corporation gets administratively dissolved.
- [ ] **Maintain Conflict of Interest Policy** — every director signs annually.
- [ ] **Maintain board meeting minutes** — at minimum one annual meeting, more if grants require documentation.

## Phase 5 — Fiscal sponsorship (interim option)

If waiting on 501(c)(3) determination and needing to accept tax-deductible donations now:

- [ ] **Identify a fiscal sponsor** (an existing 501(c)(3) that agrees to receive donations on S&S's behalf in exchange for a ~5–10% administrative fee)
      Candidates to approach:
  - Florida Nonprofit Alliance member orgs
  - Local community foundation (e.g., Community Foundation of Brevard)
  - Fractured Atlas (arts-focused, national)
- [ ] **Execute a written fiscal sponsorship agreement** — Model A (comprehensive) or Model C (pre-approved grant relationship). Model C is simpler for short-term arrangements.
- [ ] **Disclose fiscal sponsor status** to all donors in writing.
- [ ] **Transition to direct receipt** once IRS determination letter arrives.
