# Section 26: Accountability — Owning Your Story

## The Three Layers

**Layer 1: Acknowledgment** — "I did ___. It caused ___."

**Layer 2: Understanding** — Why did you do it? Not to excuse — to understand and prevent.

**Layer 3: Action** — What are you doing about it? What have you changed?

## Accountability vs. Excuses

| Excuse | Accountability |
|--------|---------------|
| "I only did it because..." | "I did it. The reason doesn't change the impact." |
| "Everyone does it." | "I'm responsible for my choices." |
| "The system set me up." | "The system has problems AND I made choices." |
| "It wasn't that bad." | "The person I hurt gets to decide how bad it was." |

## Journal Prompts
1. Complete all three layers for the action that led to your incarceration.
2. Who did you harm? Write a letter to them — not to send, but to practice accountability.
3. Describe the difference between who you were and who you are becoming.
