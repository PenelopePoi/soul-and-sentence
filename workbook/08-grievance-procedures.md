# Section 8: Grievance Procedures — How to File & Win

## Why Grievances Matter

- Create an official record
- Put the facility on notice
- Satisfy PLRA exhaustion requirement
- Can lead to actual policy changes
- Demonstrate willingness to follow proper channels

## Florida DOC Grievance Process

1. **Informal Resolution** — Try to resolve directly with staff. Document the attempt.
2. **Formal Grievance** — Submit written grievance within 15 days of incident.
3. **Response** — Warden responds within established time frame.
4. **Appeal** — If denied, appeal to Secretary of DOC.
5. **Final Decision** — Secretary's decision is the final administrative decision.

## 10 Tips

1. File on time
2. Be specific: who, what, when, where, how
3. Reference specific rules, policies, or laws
4. Stick to facts — remove emotions and insults
5. Request specific relief
6. Keep copies of everything
7. File each issue as a separate grievance
8. If rejected for procedural reasons, correct and refile
9. No response may constitute exhaustion — document it
10. Do not threaten staff or use profanity

## Sample Language

"On [date], at approximately [time], Officer [Name/Badge #] [describe what happened]. This action violated [specific policy/rule/right]. I am requesting [specific remedy]. I attempted informal resolution on [date] by [describe attempt]. The issue was not resolved."

## Journal Prompts

1. Have you ever filed a grievance? What happened?
2. Practice writing a grievance using the sample language above.
3. Why does the system require grievances before lawsuits? Is this fair?
