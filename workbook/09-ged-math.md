# Section 9: GED Preparation — Math Foundations

## Number Operations (PEMDAS)
Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication/Division (left to right), Addition/Subtraction (left to right)

Example: 3 + 4 x 2 = 3 + 8 = 11

## Fractions
- Add/subtract: find common denominator
- Multiply: numerators together, denominators together
- Divide: flip second fraction and multiply

## Decimals and Percentages
- Fraction to decimal: divide numerator by denominator
- Decimal to percentage: multiply by 100
- Percentage of a number: convert to decimal, multiply (25% of 80 = 0.25 x 80 = 20)

## Basic Algebra
If 3x + 5 = 20: subtract 5 (3x = 15), divide by 3 (x = 5)

## Geometry Basics
- Area of rectangle = length x width
- Area of triangle = 1/2 x base x height
- Area of circle = pi x r squared
- Perimeter of rectangle = 2(length + width)
- Volume of box = length x width x height

## Practice Problems
1. What is 15% of 200? (Answer: 30)
2. Solve for x: 2x - 7 = 13 (Answer: x = 10)
3. Area of 8ft x 5ft rectangle? (Answer: 40 sq ft)
4. Convert 3/4 to decimal (Answer: 0.75)
5. 3 + 2 x 5 - 1 = ? (Answer: 12)
6. Circle with radius 7, area? (Answer: 153.86 sq in)
