# Section 4: All 27 Amendments — The Full Constitution

Every American should know every amendment. This is your country's operating manual.

## Bill of Rights (1791)

| Amendment | Summary |
|-----------|---------|
| I | Freedom of speech, religion, press, assembly, petition |
| II | Right to bear arms (note: felony convictions prohibit firearm possession) |
| III | No quartering soldiers in homes |
| IV | Protection from unreasonable search and seizure |
| V | Grand jury, double jeopardy, self-incrimination, due process |
| VI | Right to counsel, speedy trial, jury, confront witnesses |
| VII | Jury trial in civil cases over $20 |
| VIII | No excessive bail, fines, or cruel and unusual punishment |
| IX | Rights not listed still exist |
| X | Powers not given to federal government belong to states or people |

## Later Amendments

| Amendment | Year | Summary |
|-----------|------|---------|
| XI | 1795 | Sovereign immunity — sue officials, not the state |
| XII | 1804 | Separate electoral votes for President and VP |
| XIII | 1865 | Abolishes slavery — except as punishment for crime |
| XIV | 1868 | Citizenship, due process, equal protection |
| XV | 1870 | Voting rights regardless of race |
| XVI | 1913 | Federal income tax |
| XVII | 1913 | Direct election of senators |
| XVIII | 1919 | Prohibition (repealed by XXI) |
| XIX | 1920 | Women's right to vote |
| XX | 1933 | Presidential term start dates |
| XXI | 1933 | Repeal of Prohibition |
| XXII | 1951 | Two-term presidential limit |
| XXIII | 1961 | D.C. electoral votes |
| XXIV | 1964 | No poll taxes |
| XXV | 1967 | Presidential succession |
| XXVI | 1971 | Voting age 18 |
| XXVII | 1992 | Congressional pay raises delayed |
