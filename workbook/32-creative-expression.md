# Section 32: Creative Expression as Healing

## How Creativity Heals
Creative expression accesses the right brain directly — where trauma is stored. Studies show it reduces cortisol, activates rest-and-restore mode, increases dopamine, and reduces symptoms of PTSD, depression, and anxiety.

## Poetry Forms
- **Haiku** — 3 lines: 5-7-5 syllables
- **Free Verse** — No rules, just truth in broken lines
- **Spoken Word** — Poetry performed aloud with rhythm and repetition

## Drawing Exercises
1. Draw your mood (abstract shapes, not faces)
2. Draw your environment in detail
3. Draw your hands
4. Draw two versions of yourself
5. Draw your dream for the future

## Songwriting Structure
Verse (tells story) → Chorus (main message, repeats) → Bridge (shift in perspective)
