# Section 11: GED Preparation — Science Fundamentals

## Life Science (40%)
- **The Cell** — Basic unit of life. Prokaryotic (bacteria, no nucleus) and Eukaryotic (animals/plants, has nucleus)
- **DNA** — Double helix carrying genetic instructions. Half from each parent.
- **Evolution** — Natural selection: traits better suited to environment survive and reproduce

## Physical Science (40%)
- **Energy** — Cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed. Types: kinetic, potential, thermal, chemical, electrical, nuclear, light
- **Newton's Laws:** (1) Objects at rest stay at rest (2) F = ma (3) Equal and opposite reaction
- **Periodic Table** — 118 elements organized by atomic number

## Earth & Space Science (20%)
- Earth is ~4.5 billion years old. Layers: crust, mantle, core
- Plate tectonics causes earthquakes and volcanic activity

## Reading Scientific Data
1. Read title and labels first
2. Identify what is measured on each axis
3. Look for trends
4. Read scales carefully
5. Draw conclusions from data, not assumptions

## Journal Prompts
1. If you could study any scientific topic, what would it be?
2. Newton's first law: an object in motion stays in motion unless acted on by a force. How does this apply to your life?
3. How have you adapted since being incarcerated? What survival skills have you developed?
