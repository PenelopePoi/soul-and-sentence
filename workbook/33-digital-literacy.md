# Section 33: Digital Literacy & Technology Readiness for Reentry

## What Has Changed
- Most job applications are online
- Cash is used less (Apple Pay, Cash App, Venmo)
- GPS replaced paper maps
- AI is used everywhere
- Remote work is common

## Smartphone Basics
- Home screen holds apps
- Settings controls Wi-Fi, volume, privacy
- App Store / Google Play for new apps
- Don't click random links (scam prevention)

## Getting a Phone
- Government Lifeline Program — lifelinesupport.org
- Prepaid phones at Walmart/Target (no credit check)

## Job Applications Online
- Indeed.com — largest job search
- LinkedIn.com — professional networking
- employflorida.com — Florida workforce

## Online Banking
- Most banks have apps
- Never send money to strangers
- Set up online bill pay to prevent late fees

## Avoiding Scams
- Phishing: Don't click links from unknown senders
- "You've won" = always fake
- Jobs asking for money upfront = scam
- Government never demands gift card payment

## Free Learning
- Khan Academy — khanacademy.org
- GCFGlobal — gcfglobal.org
- Your local public library

## Journal Prompts
1. What technology are you most nervous about?
2. What digital skill would help you most after release?
3. Write about a time technology changed your life.
