# Section 5: Florida Case Law — Key Decisions That Affect You

## Graham v. Florida, 560 U.S. 48 (2010)
Juvenile offenders cannot be sentenced to life without parole for non-homicide offenses.

## Miller v. Alabama, 567 U.S. 460 (2012)
Mandatory life without parole for juvenile homicide offenders is unconstitutional. Courts must consider age and individual circumstances.

## Hurst v. Florida, 577 U.S. 92 (2016)
Florida's death sentencing scheme was unconstitutional — a jury, not a judge, must find facts necessary for death sentence.

## Florida Amendment 4 (2018)
Restored voting rights to most former felons upon completion of all terms of their sentence.

## Florida Statute 944.275 — Gain Time
Basic gain time: 10 days per month. Florida's 85% rule means you must serve at least 85% of your sentence for most offenses.

## Florida Statute 945.091 — Supervised Community Release
Eligibility depends on offense type, sentence length, disciplinary history, and program participation.

## How to Research Florida Case Law
- Request access to the facility's law library
- Use Florida Statutes Online
- File requests for specific cases through the librarian
- Contact Florida Institutional Legal Services (FILS)
