# Section 27: Mental Health — Understanding Your Mind

## Common Conditions
- **Depression** — Persistent sadness, hopelessness, loss of interest
- **Anxiety** — Excessive worry, restlessness, racing thoughts
- **PTSD** — Flashbacks, nightmares, hypervigilance
- **Bipolar** — Extreme mood swings between mania and depression
- **ADHD** — Difficulty focusing, impulsivity (often undiagnosed in adults)

## Coping Strategies That Work
- **Routine** — Structure reduces anxiety
- **Exercise** — One of the most effective treatments
- **Writing** — Reduces symptoms of depression, anxiety, PTSD
- **Breathing** — 4-7-8 technique (inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8)
- **Connection** — Talk to someone
- **Medication** — If prescribed, take as directed. Not weakness — medical treatment.

## If You Are in Crisis
- Tell a corrections officer, counselor, or medical staff immediately
- After release: 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988)

## Journal Prompts
1. Rate your mental health 1-10. Explain why.
2. What coping mechanism do you use most? Is it healthy or unhealthy?
3. What would you tell someone on the outside about what incarceration does to mental health?
