# Section 31: Trauma-Informed Conflict Resolution

## Four Automatic Responses to Perceived Threat
- **Fight** — Aggression, dominance, control
- **Flight** — Avoidance, withdrawal, isolation
- **Freeze** — Shutdown, numbness, inability to speak
- **Fawn** — People-pleasing, over-apologizing

## Reacting vs. Responding

| Reacting | Responding |
|----------|-----------|
| Automatic | Intentional |
| Driven by emotion | Informed by emotion, guided by reason |
| Based on past trauma | Based on present reality |
| Often regretted | Often respected |

## De-Escalation Techniques
1. Lower your voice
2. Open your hands
3. Acknowledge their feeling
4. Use "I" statements
5. Ask a question instead of making a statement
6. Offer an exit
7. Never corner someone

## Communication Formula
"I feel ___ when ___ because ___."

## Journal Prompts
1. Which response is your default — fight, flight, freeze, or fawn?
2. Rewrite a conflict you handled badly using de-escalation techniques.
3. What difficult conflict are you currently avoiding?
