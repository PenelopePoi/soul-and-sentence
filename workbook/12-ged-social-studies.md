# Section 12: GED Preparation — Social Studies & Civics

## Three Branches of Government
- **Legislative (Congress)** — Makes laws. Senate (100) + House (435)
- **Executive (President)** — Enforces laws
- **Judicial (Courts)** — Interprets laws. Supreme Court is highest

## Checks and Balances
- Congress can override veto (2/3 vote)
- President can veto laws
- Supreme Court can declare laws unconstitutional
- Congress can impeach president or judges
- President appoints Supreme Court justices (confirmed by Senate)

## U.S. History Highlights
1776: Declaration of Independence | 1865: 13th Amendment | 1920: 19th Amendment | 1954: Brown v. Board | 1964: Civil Rights Act | 1965: Voting Rights Act

## Economics Basics
- Supply up = price down; Demand up = price up
- GDP: total value of goods/services produced
- Inflation: prices rise, money buys less

3. How does understanding economics help you plan for life after release?
