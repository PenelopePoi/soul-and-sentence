# Section 6: Civil Rights — 42 U.S.C. Section 1983 Claims

## What Section 1983 Says

Every person who, under color of state law, subjects any citizen to the deprivation of any rights secured by the Constitution, shall be liable to the party injured.

## What You Need to Prove

1. **State Action** — The violator was acting under color of state law
2. **Constitutional Violation** — They deprived you of a constitutional right

## Common Section 1983 Claims

- Excessive force (8th Amendment)
- Deliberate indifference to medical needs (8th Amendment)
- Failure to protect (8th Amendment)
- Retaliation for filing grievances (1st Amendment)
- Denial of access to courts (1st/14th Amendment)
- Conditions of confinement (8th Amendment)
- Religious freedom violations (1st Amendment/RLUIPA)

## Before Filing: Exhaust Administrative Remedies

Under the Prison Litigation Reform Act (PLRA), you must exhaust all available grievance procedures before filing.

## Filing a Section 1983 Complaint

1. Exhaust all grievance procedures (keep copies)
2. Obtain complaint form from law library or clerk
3. File in U.S. District Court
4. File IFP application if you cannot afford filing fee
5. Name specific individuals, not the institution
6. Describe the specific constitutional right violated
7. State what relief you are seeking

## Qualified Immunity

Government officials may claim qualified immunity unless their conduct violated "clearly established" constitutional rights.
