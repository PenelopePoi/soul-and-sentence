# Donor Appeal Letter

For mailing or emailing individual donors. One page. Customize bracketed fields. Do not use a tear-jerker opener — the reader is a person, not a mark.

---

**Dear [First Name],**

A man writes a letter from a cell in Florida. He's had twenty-four months and a pencil stub to think about who he hurt, who hurt him, and who he wants to be. His letter doesn't ask for anything. It says one thing, clearly, for the first time: *I know what I did.*

He will mail that letter to no one. It's for him.

This is what **Soul & Sentence** builds: a private, structured writing practice for incarcerated people, at no cost to them. A 33-section workbook covering constitutional rights, GED prep, trauma-informed recovery, and reentry planning — the things the system doesn't teach and the street won't hold.

We send it directly to any incarcerated person who asks. We don't charge them. We don't charge the facility. We don't promise early release. We don't take a cut of their parole. We just hand them the tools and get out of the way.

## Why this matters now

Three facts, no hedging:

1. **Over 1.2 million people are incarcerated in U.S. state and federal prisons.** Most lack reliable access to legal education, mental-health tools, or reentry preparation.
2. **Recidivism drops sharply when structured programming exists.** Writing, legal literacy, and reentry planning — our three pillars — each correlate independently with reduced reoffense.
3. **Fiscal sponsorship is in place while 501(c)(3) determination is pending** (see below). Your donation is tax-deductible today.

## What we don't do

- We do not provide legal representation.
- We do not provide therapy.
- We do not promise parole board points.
- We do not share participant writing without written permission.
- We do not water down the work to be easier to sell.

We send honest material to people who are ready to do honest work. That's the whole model.

## What we do

- Answer every letter.

No executive director salary. Alex Weatherspoon founded this and runs it. Currently uncompensated. Future compensation, if any, will be reviewed by the board and disclosed on the annual report.

The man in the cell writing to no one is real. His letter doesn't exist because someone gave him a form and a deadline. It exists because someone handed him a pencil and said: *be honest. Nobody is going to grade this. Just tell the truth once, for yourself.*
