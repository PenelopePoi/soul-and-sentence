# Anthology Submission Guidelines

## What the Anthology Is

The Soul & Sentence anthology is a published annual collection of writing by incarcerated individuals. It is distributed to reentry programs, libraries, universities, and the general public. Proceeds fund free workbook distribution to more facilities.

Submission is **entirely voluntary**. Your writing is yours first. You decide whether it becomes a bridge to someone outside.

## Who Can Submit

Anyone currently or formerly incarcerated in any U.S. federal, state, or local correctional facility. No age limit. No citizenship requirement. No genre restriction.

## What We Accept

- **Poetry** — any form, any length
- **Prose** — essays, memoir, letters (real or imagined), short fiction
- **Hybrid** — lyric essays, mixed-form work, verse-prose blends

Word count: no minimum. Maximum 4,000 words per piece. Up to 5 pieces per submission window.

## What We Look For

- Honesty over polish
- Specificity over generality — one concrete image beats ten abstractions
- Voice — writing that sounds like *you*, not what you think an anthology wants
- Truth, in whatever form it takes

## What We Don't Publish

- Writing that names living private individuals (co-defendants, victims, staff) without consent
- Graphic content glorifying violence against specific, identifiable people
- Promotional content for commercial products or services
- Writing that threatens specific individuals

If your submission hits one of these, we'll ask you to revise. Most pieces are fine.

## How to Submit

### By mail (primary method)

Send to:

```
Soul & Sentence — Anthology Submissions
766 Lunar Lake Circle
Cocoa, FL 32922
```

Include on a separate sheet (or the first page):

- Your full name (use a pen name only if you must — explain why)
- Facility and inmate ID (we use this to verify and to reply)
- Date of submission
- Titles of pieces enclosed
- A one-sentence bio (who you are, in your words)
- A sentence on whether you want your real name, first name only, initials, or a pen name printed

### By email (for released or transitional writers)

[soulnsentence@gmail.com](mailto:soulnsentence@gmail.com) — subject line *"Anthology Submission — [Your Name]"*.

## What Happens Next

1. We read every submission. Usually within 30 days; sometimes longer during anthology-close.
2. You get a reply either way — accepted, not this cycle, or revision request.
3. If accepted, we mail you a **contributor release form** (see below) and a galley proof once typesetting is done.
4. You retain full copyright. We publish under a one-time anthology license. You can publish the piece anywhere else, anytime, with no permission needed from us.

## Contributor Release Form (summary)

The full form ships with acceptance letters. In plain language it says:

- You grant Soul & Sentence one-time rights to publish the piece in the anthology and in related promotional excerpts (no more than 100 words)
- You keep your copyright forever — this is a **license**, not a sale
- You can revoke permission for future editions any time in writing
- You agree the work is yours and doesn't infringe anyone else's rights
- If you want payment to go to family instead of commissary, we can do that — ask

## Compensation

Contributors receive:

- 3 physical copies of the anthology (mailed to the facility if rules allow, or to a family member)
- A small honorarium per published piece, directly to your commissary account or to a family member you designate (the exact amount is set at the start of each cycle — currently $25 per piece)
- Listing in the contributors page

## Questions

Write us. We reply to every letter. Even "I'm not ready yet" letters. Even "I wrote this once and I hate it now" letters. Especially those.
