Dear Warden [LAST NAME],

I am writing to offer your facility free access to **Soul & Sentence — Redemption by the Pen**, a creative writing program for incarcerated individuals. Our 33-section workbook combines legal education, GED preparation, therapeutic reflection, and reentry planning with a structured writing practice. We send materials directly to the individuals who request them — no fees, no paperwork, no obligation on your part.

## What we provide at no cost to the facility

- **The workbook** (physical or digital) — 33 sections, covering constitutional rights, search-and-seizure law, due process, Florida case law, civil rights (§1983), grievance procedures, GED prep, shadow work, inner child healing, addiction recovery, anger management, reentry planning, creative expression, and trauma-informed conflict resolution
- **Writing prompts** — 12 structured prompts plus 3 reflection exercises, designed for self-paced work
- **Anthology inclusion** — participants may voluntarily submit writing for our published anthology of incarcerated writers
- **Resource directory** — 30+ legal aid, reentry, and mental health organizations with contact information

## What we ask

- **Permission to correspond** with individuals who request materials (standard incoming-mail channel is fine)
- **Optional:** a designated contact person (chaplain, education coordinator, or programs officer) for logistical coordination
- **Optional:** permission to facilitate a writing circle in-facility (where group programming exists). No one is required to participate. Attendance is voluntary.

## What the program does NOT do

- It does not promise sentence reduction, gain time, or parole board consideration
- It does not offer legal advice or representation
- It does not provide mental-health treatment
- It does not criticize, grade, or require any specific writing output
- It does not require participants to share their work

## Why this matters to your facility

Research on correctional programming consistently shows that structured writing, legal literacy, and reentry preparation reduce recidivism and improve institutional conduct. The Soul & Sentence workbook meets all three in one package, at zero cost to your budget.

Please let me know whether you are open to receiving a program packet for review, or if you would prefer a brief call to discuss the logistics. I can be reached at the contact information above.

Thank you for your time and for the work you do.

## Follow-up protocol (internal, not part of the letter)

- Wait 14 days for response
- Second contact: phone call to facility main line, ask for Programs Office or Chaplain's Office
- Third contact: certified mail follow-up with delivery receipt
- If declined: thank them, document the facility, move on. Do not argue. Some facilities take 3+ attempts over years.
