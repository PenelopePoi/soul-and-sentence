# Soul & Sentence: Redemption by the Pen

> *"Your sentence is time. Your soul is yours. Use the pen to find what the world tried to take from you."*
> -- Alex Weatherspoon

## About

**Soul & Sentence** is a creative writing and education program for incarcerated individuals. We believe that writing is the most honest form of self-examination, and that every person -- regardless of their charge, their past, or their sentence -- has a story worth telling and a truth worth finding.

This repository contains the complete **33-section workbook** covering legal education, GED preparation, therapeutic healing, creative expression, and reentry planning. All materials are provided **free of charge** to incarcerated individuals and correctional facilities.

## Our Mission

We send writing prompts and workbook materials directly to people inside correctional facilities. There is no enrollment fee. There is no application. There is no waitlist.

## What We Believe

- That writing is a form of freedom that exists within any walls
- That every human being is more than their worst moment
- That accountability and self-worth can coexist
- That creative expression is not a luxury -- it is a survival tool
- That truth told on paper has the power to heal the person who wrote it
