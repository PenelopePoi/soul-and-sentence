# Soul & Sentence: Redemption by the Pen

> *"Your sentence is time. Your soul is yours. Use the pen to find what the world tried to take from you."*
> — Alex Weatherspoon

## About

**Soul & Sentence** is a creative writing and education program for incarcerated individuals. We believe that writing is the most honest form of self-examination, and that every person — regardless of their charge, their past, or their sentence — has a story worth telling and a truth worth finding.

All program materials are provided **free of charge** to incarcerated individuals and correctional facilities.

## The Workbook — 33 Sections

| Part | Sections | Topics |
|------|----------|--------|
| **The Program** | Mission, Prompts, Exercises | 12 writing prompts, 3 reflection exercises |
| **I: Legal Education** | 1-8 | Constitutional rights, due process, amendments, Florida case law, civil rights (§1983), pro se filing, grievances |
| **II: GED Preparation** | 9-12 | Math, language arts, science, social studies |
| **III: Legal Glossary** | 13 | 100+ essential legal terms |
| **IV: Therapeutic Growth** | 14-20 | Shadow work, inner child, parenting, faith, recovery, anger, relationships |
| **V: Reentry Preparation** | 21-23 | Financial literacy, job readiness, transition planning |
| **VI: Legal Process** | 24-25 | Bail & bonds, sentence computation |
| **VII: Accountability & Mental Health** | 26-27 | Owning your story, understanding your mind |
| **VIII: Resources** | 28-29 | Organizations, phone numbers, hotlines |
| **IX: Reflection** | 30 | Personal commitment statement |
| **X: New Sections** | 31-33 | Conflict resolution, creative expression, digital literacy |

## Our Mission

We send writing prompts and workbook materials directly to people inside correctional facilities. There is no enrollment fee. There is no application. There is no waitlist.

## What We Believe

- Writing is a form of freedom that exists within any walls
- Every human being is more than their worst moment
- Accountability and self-worth can coexist
- The people closest to the pain are the most qualified to write about it
- Creative expression is not a luxury — it is a survival tool
- Truth told on paper has the power to heal the person who wrote it
